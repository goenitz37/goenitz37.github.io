Stock Research Drive Sync

Stock Research Drive Sync is a small personal automation used by a single individual, the developer, for the developer's own stock market research. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it is not offered to other people.

What the application does

On a schedule, the application downloads daily stock price history from a public market-data provider and saves it as CSV files in the developer's own Google Drive. It also reads, in read-only mode, one Google Sheet owned by the developer that lists which stock symbols to track.

Google user data it accesses

  • Google Drive: the application reads and writes only the stock price CSV files and one small sync-state file in the developer's own Drive folders. The full Drive scope is requested because those folders were created outside of this application.
  • Google Sheets: read-only access to the developer's own symbol list.

Only the Google account of the developer is ever authorized. No other person's Google data is accessed, collected, stored, or shared.
